Hours of Operation

Live

This holds the hours this page publishes, to the minute. It opens empty, because the only hours published here ran to 8 August. Press the back arrow twice, to July, and the pool fills up.

Once there are sessions on screen, tap Lap Swim or Rec Swim in the filter to see one of them on its own.

It opens empty, and that is not a fault in the calendar. The only hours published for this pool are headed “Summer Operational Hours: June 14, 2026 — August 8, 2026”. That season ended, so a month after it there is nothing to draw. Press the back arrow twice, to July, and the pool fills up — every lap swim and rec swim session, exactly as the page lists them.
  • The pool is year-round, and the city's own Year-Round Pools list shows it OPEN, HEATED today.
  • The Citywide Aquatics page says year-round hours “vary per facility” and to see the year-round list for detail. That list carries statuses and addresses, and no hours at all.
  • So a swimmer who wants to know when they can swim this week has nowhere on the site to find out. That is the gap, and it is the only thing here worth fixing.

Pool status

Expo Family Pool is open for lap and recreational swimming. Expo Competition Pool remains closed for maintenance. Both are in the calendar above; the Competition Pool holds no sessions, which is what closed looks like.

What this replaces

  • A season that has to be retyped to end. The hours are headed with a date range, so when the range passes somebody has to go in and replace the block. Until they do, the page confidently publishes hours that stopped over a month ago. A calendar holds the end date itself and simply stops.
  • Two answers to the same question. The holiday list says December 31 is closed. The Holiday Half Days list says December 31 is open 1pm–5pm. Only one of those can be true, and a calendar can only hold one of them.
  • “MLKD/Juneteenth (January 19)”. Two different holidays, six months apart, on one line with one date.
  • A link called City Swimming Pool Report that opens a report from 2004. Nothing on the page says how old any of it is; a dated calendar always does.
  • Hours typed once per pool, on 26 year-round pages. Every one of them ages the same way, and each has to be found and edited by hand.
